Privacy Policy

Météo Québec — last updated: August 6, 2026

This English text is provided for convenience only. The service is offered to the public in Québec, where the French version is the official one. In case of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

Météo Québec is a personal, free, ad-free project hosted in Québec and intended for a Québec audience. It requires no account, keeps no user profile, and uses neither cookies nor advertising trackers.

This policy explains in plain language what the application actually does with your information. It describes how the code behaves, and that code is public and verifiable.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no passwords.
  • No database: nothing about you is stored on our servers.
  • No analytics tools (no Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible or similar).
  • No cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising trackers.
  • No access to your device's location. The application never requests it, and the server explicitly blocks it through the Permissions-Policy header.
  • No selling, no sharing for commercial or advertising purposes.

What stays on your device

Your preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to our servers and never leave your device.

Key Contents
selection The city you selected, or an indication that you are using a postal code.
codePostal The postal code you entered.
lieuCP The place matching that postal code: the first three characters of the postal code, the municipality name, the province, and the geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of that place.
unite Your unit preference, metric or imperial.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the Android application. Nothing remains on our side.

What your device sends to our servers

When you view a forecast, your device asks our server for data about a place: either the identifier of one of the offered cities, or geographic coordinates, or the first three characters of a postal code. These requests contain no personal identifier.

Server logs

Our server writes one line per request, containing:

  • a random technical request identifier, unconnected to you;
  • the method and requested path, truncated to 200 characters;
  • the response status code and its duration.

Your IP address does not appear in these logs. The requested path, however, may contain the first three characters of a postal code or geographic coordinates, since those are address parameters. These logs are used solely for technical diagnostics. They rotate automatically and are capped by volume: the oldest entries are discarded as new ones arrive.

IP address

Your IP address is processed in memory only, long enough to check that the number of requests stays reasonable and to protect the service from abuse. It is not logged, not stored, and does not survive a server restart.

Server cache

Weather responses are cached in memory to limit calls to data providers. That cache holds forecasts per location — never information about people — is capped in size, and is lost on every restart.

Third-party services

Contacted by our server

In these cases our server calls the provider. Your IP address is not passed along: the provider only sees ours.

Service What is sent
Open-Meteo (Germany) Latitude, longitude and time zone of the requested place.
Zippopotam.us The first three characters of the postal code, never the full code.
RainViewer The list of available radar frames. No data about you.

Contacted directly by your browser

The animated map is the exception, and it is the most important point in this section. Map tiles are loaded directly by your device, without passing through our servers. These providers therefore see your IP address and the geographic area you are viewing, and their own policies apply.

Service Role
CARTO (base map, data from OpenStreetMap) The dark base map beneath the precipitation layer.
RainViewer Satellite and radar image tiles.

If you would rather avoid these exchanges, simply do not scroll down to the map: tiles are only requested once it is displayed. The rest of the application works normally.

Infrastructure and distribution

  • Cloudflare (United States) provides the tunnel and HTTPS encryption through which the site is reachable. As such, all requests transit its network.
  • Google Play distributes the Android version. Its installation and updates fall under Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Offline behaviour

The application is installable and keeps on your device, through a service worker, the most recent forecasts you viewed along with a limited number of map tiles (up to 200, for at most seven days). This cache lives solely on your device and is cleared along with site data.

Android notifications

The Android version declares the notification permission. As of today, the application sends no notifications: the permission is reserved for possible future weather alerts. Should that feature be added, this policy will be updated before it goes live, and notifications will remain refusable from Android settings.

Your rights

This service is subject to Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Law 25, and to the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Since no personal information is kept on our servers, there is in practice no record to access, correct or delete. Your rights work as follows:

  • Access and deletion — everything concerning you sits in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data erases it permanently.
  • Questions and complaints — write to contact@alithiel31.dev.

The person responsible for the protection of personal information is the service's publisher, reachable at that same address.

If a response does not satisfy you, you may contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec. Residents of the European Economic Area retain the rights granted to them by the GDPR; the service, however, does not target them and is not specifically intended for them.

Hosting and transfers outside Québec

The service is hosted in Québec, on a personal server. Some providers named above are established outside the province — notably Open-Meteo in Germany and Cloudflare in the United States — and may process there the technical requests described in this policy.

Children

The application is intended for the general public. It knowingly collects no information from children, and in fact collects no personal information from anyone.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. The site applies a strict Content Security Policy restricting which domains may load resources. The strongest protection, however, is structural: information that is never collected cannot leak.

Changes

This policy may evolve alongside the application. The date at the top of this page is authoritative, and the full history of changes is public in the project repository.
